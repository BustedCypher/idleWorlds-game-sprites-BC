# Environment-focused zone header prompt set

Generated with the built-in image generator. Every image used the approved
uncropped Greenwake Den master as the series-style reference and the matching
construction building artwork (or its atlas sprite) as a secondary architecture
reference.

## Shared prompt

- Premium stylized, hand-painted high-fantasy strategy-game environment art.
- Match the Greenwake reference's rendering polish, atmosphere, rich material
  detail, controlled saturation, and panoramic master format.
- The zone is the hero: 75–85% of the visual impression comes from landscape,
  geology, vegetation, water, sky, weather, and depth.
- Buildings collectively occupy roughly 15–20% of the scene. Use several small,
  readable structures integrated into the world; no dominant close-up landmark.
- Very distant wide establishing viewpoint; broad foreground, expansive
  midground, deep background, detailed edge-to-edge scenery, and a crop-safe
  central band for website UI.
- No text, logos, watermark, border, UI, modern objects, isolated-icon framing,
  transparent areas, or prominent characters.
